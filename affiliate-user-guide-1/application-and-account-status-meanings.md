# Application and Account Status Meanings
### Application Status
- Approved - Application is approved.
- Declined - Application is declined.
- Change Requested - An Action has been sent to the Affilate to comply and is under further review before approval is granted.
### Account Status
- In Good Standing - Affiliate is approved they have paid or they have been Re-activated, they have complete access.
- Pending Invoice - Affiliate has access to the account but can not download, once payment is received, Admin places account In Good Standing.
- De-Activation Pending - Flags an Affiliate account but they can not download releases, they have a certain time to respond to an IHTSDO action. They have access to their account.
- De-Activated - Affiliate Account is De-Activated for failure of payment or other infraction; they have no Access to download and have no access to their MLDS account. The reason for De-activation is noted to Affiliate.
